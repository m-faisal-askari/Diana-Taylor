About Diana

Diana Taylor works in the space between what is said and what is withheld.

Trained for the stage and drawn to the camera, she builds characters from the inside out - favouring restraint, contradiction, and the small gestures that betray a whole interior life. Directors return to her for the same reason audiences do: she makes silence legible.

She lives between projects and cities, and reads more than she sleeps.
